MORTGAGE COMPLIANCE RULES — KNOWLEDGE BASE
==========================================
Last updated: 2024-01-01
Source: Federal lending regulations, CFPB guidelines, internal policy

---

SECTION 1: DEBT-TO-INCOME RATIO (DTI)
--------------------------------------
Rule: The borrower's total monthly debt obligations divided by gross monthly income
must not exceed 43% for Qualified Mortgage (QM) status.
- Front-end DTI (housing costs only): Should not exceed 28%
- Back-end DTI (all debts): Hard cap at 43% for QM; up to 50% with compensating factors
- Compensating factors that allow higher DTI: significant cash reserves (12+ months PITI),
  low LTV (< 70%), strong credit score (> 740)

Action required: Calculate and document both front-end and back-end DTI.
Red flag: DTI > 43% with no compensating factors = automatic compliance review required.

---

SECTION 2: LOAN-TO-VALUE RATIO (LTV)
--------------------------------------
Rule: LTV is the loan amount divided by the lesser of appraised value or purchase price.
- Conventional loans: Max LTV 97% (requires PMI if > 80%)
- FHA loans: Max LTV 96.5% (580+ credit score) or 90% (500-579 credit score)
- VA loans: Up to 100% LTV (no PMI required)
- USDA loans: Up to 100% LTV in eligible rural areas
- Jumbo loans: Max LTV typically 80-85%

Action required: Order appraisal from approved AMC (Appraisal Management Company).
Red flag: Purchase price significantly above appraised value — potential fraud risk.

SECTION 3: REQUIRED DOCUMENT CHECKLIST
----------------------------------------
All applications must include:
  [ ] Completed 1003 Uniform Residential Loan Application
  [ ] Government-issued photo ID (driver's license or passport)
  [ ] Social Security card or ITIN documentation
  [ ] Last 2 years W-2 forms (all employers)
  [ ] Last 2 years signed federal tax returns (all pages, all schedules)
  [ ] Last 30 days pay stubs (most recent)
  [ ] Last 2 months bank statements (all pages, all accounts)
  [ ] Proof of down payment source (gift letter if applicable)
  [ ] Purchase agreement (for purchase loans)
  [ ] Current mortgage statement (for refinances)
  [ ] HOA documents (if applicable)
  [ ] Self-employed borrowers: 2 years business tax returns + YTD P&L

Red flag: Any altered, whited-out, or inconsistent documents — refer to fraud unit.

---

SECTION 4: EMPLOYMENT AND INCOME VERIFICATION
-----------------------------------------------
Rule: Borrower must demonstrate 2-year employment history.
- W-2 employees: Verify via pay stubs + W-2s + verbal VOE (Verification of Employment)
- Self-employed: 2 years business returns; income averaged over 2 years
- Commission/bonus income: Averaged over 2 years; must have 2-year history
- Part-time income: Acceptable if 2-year history and likely to continue
- Rental income: 75% of gross rents (to account for vacancy); Schedule E documentation required
- Gaps in employment: Less than 6 months acceptable; greater than 6 months requires letter of explanation

---

SECTION 5: CREDIT SCORE REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------
- Conventional: Minimum 620 (660+ for better pricing)
- FHA: Minimum 580 for 96.5% LTV; 500-579 for 90% LTV
- VA: No official minimum; lender typically requires 620
- USDA: Minimum 640
- Jumbo: Typically minimum 720

Note: Use the middle score of the three bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion).
For multiple borrowers: use the lower of the two middle scores.

---

SECTION 6: FAIR LENDING — ECOA AND HMDA
-----------------------------------------
ECOA (Equal Credit Opportunity Act) requires:
- Do not discriminate based on: race, color, religion, national origin, sex,
  marital status, age, receipt of public assistance, or exercise of consumer rights.
- If denying credit: Issue Adverse Action Notice within 30 days with specific reasons.
- Cannot ask about plans for children or family status.

HMDA (Home Mortgage Disclosure Act) requires:
- Collect and report race, ethnicity, sex, and income data on all applications.
- Data is reported annually to CFPB.
- Do not use this data in credit decisions.

Red flag: Any denial reason that correlates with protected class characteristics
without documented creditworthiness basis → immediate compliance escalation.

SECTION 7: QUALIFIED MORTGAGE (QM) SAFE HARBOR
-------------------------------------------------
To qualify for QM Safe Harbor, the loan must:
  1. Have a loan term of 30 years or less
  2. Have no negative amortization (balance cannot increase)
  3. Have no interest-only payments
  4. Have no balloon payments (except for certain small creditor loans)
  5. Have points and fees not exceeding 3% of the loan amount
  6. Be underwritten to the maximum scheduled payment
  7. Verify and document the borrower's income, assets, and financial obligations

Failure to meet QM requirements does not automatically disqualify the loan but
removes the lender's legal protection against "ability to repay" lawsuits.

---

SECTION 8: TRID (TILA-RESPA INTEGRATED DISCLOSURE)
----------------------------------------------------
- Loan Estimate (LE): Must be delivered within 3 business days of application.
- Closing Disclosure (CD): Must be delivered at least 3 business days before closing.
- Changes that require new LE: APR increase > 1/8%, loan product change, prepayment penalty added.
- Keep all disclosure delivery records (timestamps, method of delivery).
