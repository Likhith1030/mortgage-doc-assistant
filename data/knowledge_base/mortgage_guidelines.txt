INTERNAL MORTGAGE PROCESSING GUIDELINES
=======================================
Version 3.2 | Retail Lending Division

---

LOAN TYPES AND BASIC PARAMETERS
---------------------------------
Conventional (Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac):
  - Loan limits: $766,550 (single family, 2024); higher in high-cost areas
  - Occupancy: Primary, secondary, investment
  - PMI required if LTV > 80%

FHA (Federal Housing Administration):
  - Loan limits vary by county (check HUD website)
  - Upfront MIP: 1.75% of loan amount (can be financed)
  - Annual MIP: 0.55%–1.05% depending on LTV and term
  - Occupancy: Primary residence only

VA (Veterans Affairs):
  - Available to eligible veterans, active duty, and surviving spouses
  - Funding fee: 1.25%–3.3% (waived if service-connected disability)
  - No PMI, no minimum down payment
  - Certificate of Eligibility (COE) required

USDA (Rural Development):
  - Property must be in USDA-eligible rural area
  - Guarantee fee: 1% upfront + 0.35% annual
  - Income limits apply (usually 115% of area median income)

Jumbo:
  - Loan amount above conforming limit
  - Portfolio products — kept on bank's balance sheet
  - Stricter underwriting: typically 720+ credit, 80% LTV max, 12 months reserves

---

PROCESSING STAGES
------------------
Stage 1: APPLICATION (Day 1-3)
  - Borrower submits 1003 application
  - Pull tri-merge credit report
  - Issue Loan Estimate within 3 business days
  - Collect initial document package

Stage 2: PROCESSING (Day 3-15)
  - Processor reviews and orders missing items
  - Order appraisal
  - Order title search
  - Verify employment (VOE)
  - Upload complete file to Encompass (LOS)

Stage 3: UNDERWRITING (Day 15-25)
  - UW reviews complete package
  - Issues: Approval, Conditional Approval, Suspend, or Deny
  - Conditional approval: borrower must satisfy conditions before closing

Stage 4: CLOSING PREPARATION (Day 25-30)
  - Clear all UW conditions
  - Title insurance commitment
  - Hazard insurance binder
  - Issue Closing Disclosure 3 business days before closing

Stage 5: CLOSING AND FUNDING
  - Wet signing (or remote online notarization where permitted)
  - Wire funds
  - Record deed of trust / mortgage

---

APPRAISAL GUIDELINES
---------------------
- Must be ordered through approved AMC (Appraisal Management Company) list
- Appraiser must be state-licensed and on approved roster
- Appraisal valid for 120 days (conventional) or 180 days (FHA/VA)
- Reconsideration of Value (ROV) allowed if borrower has supporting comps
- Desktop appraisal allowed for LTV ≤ 90% with strong credit profile

---

FRAUD INDICATORS — FLAG FOR REVIEW
------------------------------------
Document fraud:
  - Altered or whited-out information on bank statements
  - Round-dollar deposits immediately before application
  - Employer phone numbers resolving to residential lines
  - Social Security numbers that don't match credit bureau data

Income fraud:
  - Income inconsistent with stated occupation
  - Tax returns with no AGI deductions (implausible)
  - Multiple jobs at same employer address

Property fraud:
  - Purchase price significantly above list price or market
  - Buyer and seller sharing same address
  - Multiple applications on same property

Action: Flag → complete SAR (Suspicious Activity Report) if fraud confirmed.

---

DOCUMENT RETENTION REQUIREMENTS
---------------------------------
- All loan files: Minimum 3 years from date of loan action
- HMDA data: 3 years
- Fair lending records: 25 months from date of action
- Electronic storage acceptable if retrievable within 24 hours
